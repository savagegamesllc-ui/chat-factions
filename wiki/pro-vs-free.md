# PRO vs FREE

Chat Factions supports two tiers:

## FREE
Includes:
- FREE overlay layouts
- core faction + meter behavior
- basic chat interaction

Best for:
- new streamers
- low-CPU setups
- minimal visual overlays

## PRO
Unlocks:
- PRO overlay layouts
- richer visuals and reactions
- deeper config controls (performance and tuning)

Best for:
- streamers who want high-impact visuals
- community-driven hype systems
- “big moment” overlays

## If you downgrade
If you lose PRO:
- PRO overlays may display a locked/disabled experience until you upgrade again
- FREE overlays remain available
