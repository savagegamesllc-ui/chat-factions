# Understanding Hype & Factions

Chat Factions is powered by **hype**.

## What Is Hype?
Hype is a live score generated by:
- Chat activity
- Viewer actions
- Twitch events (bits, subs, etc.)

Hype directly controls:
- Overlay intensity
- Visual effects
- Big “moment” reactions

---

## What Are Factions?
A **Faction** is a team your viewers join.

Each faction:
- Has a color
- Builds hype independently
- Competes visually with other factions

Some overlays:
- Blend all factions
- Highlight the leading faction
- React to sudden hype spikes

---

## FREE vs PRO Behavior
- FREE overlays use simplified hype logic
- PRO overlays unlock:
  - Advanced reactions
  - More visual layers
  - Performance tuning controls
