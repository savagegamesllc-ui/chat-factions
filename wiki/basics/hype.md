# Hype & Factions

Chat Factions is built around two core ideas:
**Hype** and **Factions**.

Understanding these will help you tune overlays and chat behavior effectively.

---

## What Is Hype?

Hype is a live numeric value that represents chat energy.

Hype can be generated by:
- Chat commands
- Viewer participation
- Twitch events (bits, subs, etc.)
- External events (via the Event API)

Overlays react to hype by:
- Increasing intensity
- Spawning more effects
- Triggering special reactions during spikes

---

## What Are Factions?

Factions are teams that viewers join.

Each faction:
- Has a name and color
- Accumulates hype independently
- Competes or blends visually with other factions

Viewers typically belong to **one faction at a time**.

---

## How Overlays Use Factions

Different overlays interpret factions in different ways:

### Weighted (Blended)
- All factions contribute
- Colors mix based on hype values

### Winner-Based
- The leading faction dominates the visuals
- Other factions may fade back

### Multi-Region
- Multiple factions appear in different screen regions

The behavior depends on the selected overlay.

---

## Hype Spikes

A hype spike is a rapid increase in hype over a short time.

Spikes can:
- Trigger flashes or bursts
- Cause dramatic visual changes
- Emphasize big stream moments

If spikes feel too strong:
- Reduce intensity
- Increase hype scaling values
- Add cooldowns or clamps in chat settings

---

## Tuning Philosophy

Good tuning balances:
- Visibility at low hype
- Excitement at high hype
- Performance stability

If an overlay ever feels overwhelming:
**reduce intensity first** — it’s the safest adjustment.
