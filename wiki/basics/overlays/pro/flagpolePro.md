# Flagpole (Pro)

A retro-inspired flagpole where hype raises a waving flag, enhanced with custom imagery and celebratory effects at peak hype.

---

## Overview

Flagpole (Pro) builds on the Flagpole overlay by allowing the streamer to replace the fabric flag with a custom image. The image is displayed in the shape of a waving flag attached to the pole, maintaining the same gentle wind motion as the Free version.

As hype builds across all factions, the flag rises higher up the pole. At very high hype levels, subtle fireworks appear in the distance, adding a celebratory backdrop without overwhelming the rest of the stream layout.

---

## Hype Behavior

Total hype across all active factions determines how high the flag is raised on the pole.

Low hype keeps the flag near the bottom. As hype increases, the flag steadily climbs upward, reaching near the top at maximum hype. Movement is smoothed to avoid sudden jumps.

The leading faction continues to control the overall visual identity of the overlay, including the color influence used for effects.

---

## Visual Behavior

The flag always waves gently, simulating a steady breeze regardless of hype level.

If a custom flag image is provided, it is fitted into the flag’s shape. Images are automatically stretched or compressed to match the flag area so that non-standard sizes still display correctly.

When hype reaches its highest levels, small fireworks appear in the background. These fireworks are intentionally subtle and distant, serving as a visual reward for peak engagement rather than a dominant effect.

---

## Customization

Streamers can adjust the flagpole’s placement, flag size, wind strength, and how quickly the flag responds to hype changes.

The Pro version also allows a custom flag image to be used in place of a solid-colored flag. Image fitting behavior can be adjusted to stretch the image to the flag area or preserve its aspect ratio while fitting within the available space.

Fireworks behavior can be enabled or disabled and tuned to control when they appear and how prominent they are.

---

## Availability

Flagpole (Pro) is available as a Pro overlay.

It includes all functionality of the Free version, with additional visual customization and celebratory effects unlocked for high-hype moments.
