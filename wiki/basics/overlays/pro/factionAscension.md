Faction Ascension

A glowing faction sigil rises upward as hype builds, becoming more intense and dramatic during big moments. It is most impactful during hype spikes where a single faction surges ahead.

Overview

Faction Ascension centers on a luminous, rune-like sigil that emerges from the lower portion of the screen and ascends as total hype increases. The visual grows in presence through layered glow, motion, and particle effects while staying compact enough to sit beneath gameplay or near a webcam.

Hype Behavior

Low hype behavior
The sigil is faint and barely visible, with minimal glow and little to no surrounding motion.

Medium hype behavior
The sigil becomes clearly visible and rises upward. Trails and particles begin to appear, adding motion and energy around the sigil.

High / spike behavior
At high hype or sharp spikes, the effect intensifies with sparks, bursts of motion, and a shockwave effect. The leading faction visually asserts dominance during these moments.

Faction Interaction

Blended / weighted
Faction colors blend together based on their relative hype contributions.

Winner-dominant
During peak hype, the leading faction’s color can take over the visual entirely.

Configuration Options

Faction Mix
Controls whether faction colors are blended together or dominated by the leading faction.

Dominance at Tier 3
Forces the leading faction’s color to take over at the highest hype level.

Intensity
Adjusts the overall strength and responsiveness of the effect.

FPS Cap
Limits the frame rate used by the overlay.

Max Particles
Controls how many particles can be on screen at once.

Hype Scale
Adjusts how quickly the overlay reacts to incoming hype.

Hype Smoothing
Controls how smoothly hype changes are applied visually.

Max Total Clamp
Caps the total hype used to drive the visuals.

Sigil X
Sets the horizontal position of the sigil.

Base Y
Sets the starting vertical position of the sigil.

Rise Height
Controls how far the sigil rises at maximum hype.

Sigil Size
Adjusts the overall size of the sigil.

Base Opacity
Controls how visible the sigil is at low hype.

Glow Strength
Adjusts the strength of the sigil’s glow.

Trail Strength
Controls the intensity of trailing particle effects.

Background Dim
Darkens the background behind the overlay as hype increases.

Enable Sparks
Toggles spark effects at higher hype levels.

Shockwave Strength
Controls the intensity of the shockwave effect during major hype spikes.

Performance Notes

This overlay uses particle effects and layered glow that scale with hype. Higher particle counts and uncapped frame rates may increase GPU usage.

Best Use Cases

Competitive or faction-driven streams

Events with frequent hype spikes

Streams where dramatic visual payoff for big moments is desired
