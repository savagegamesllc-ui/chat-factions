Flame Edge (Rock)

A band of animated flame clings to the edges of the screen, growing thicker and more energetic as hype rises, with synchronized rock music layers that intensify during big moments.

Overview

Flame Edge (Rock) frames the stream with a fiery border that flickers, glows, and sheds embers. The flames respond continuously to chat hype while an accompanying rock audio mix builds and recedes alongside the visuals.

Hype Behavior

Low hype behavior
A thin, subdued flame line with gentle motion and minimal embers. Audio presence is faint or inactive, keeping the effect restrained.

Medium hype behavior
The flame thickens and brightens, with faster motion and a steady flow of embers. Additional layers of rock audio fade in, adding energy without overwhelming the stream.

High / spike behavior
Flames surge with strong glow and rapid movement, embers burst upward, and the entire frame can shake subtly during peak moments. The full rock mix is active, creating a clear audiovisual spike.

Faction Interaction

Factions are blended / weighted. Flame color reflects a weighted mix of all active faction colors based on their current hype contribution.

Configuration Options

Position
Choose whether flames appear only along the bottom edge or wrap around the sides and bottom.

Intensity Scale
Adjusts how aggressively flame thickness and motion respond to hype.

Base Thickness
Controls the minimum flame thickness at low hype.

Max Thickness
Sets the maximum flame thickness at high hype.

Glow Blur
Adjusts the softness and spread of the flame glow.

FPS Cap
Limits the maximum frame rate for the overlay.

Render Scale
Lowers or raises internal render resolution to balance quality and performance.

DPR Cap
Caps device pixel ratio to avoid excessive rendering cost on high-DPI displays.

Hype Scale (k)
Controls how quickly hype ramps from subtle to intense.

Max Total Clamp
Limits the effective total hype used for visual scaling.

Ember Rate
Adjusts how frequently embers spawn as hype increases.

Max Embers
Caps the total number of embers on screen.

Ember Spawn Cap / Frame
Limits how many embers can spawn in a single frame.

Shockwave Strength
Controls the intensity of the visual pulse at maximum hype.

Max Shake
Sets how much the frame can shake during peak hype.

Audio Enabled
Turns the rock audio layers on or off.

Audio Pack ID
Selects which rock audio pack is used.

Audio Master Volume
Overall volume for the overlay audio.

Audio Fade In
How quickly audio layers fade in as hype rises.

Audio Fade Out
How quickly audio layers fade out as hype drops.

Performance Notes

This overlay includes adjustable limits for frame rate, render scale, pixel ratio, and particle counts to help manage performance on lower-powered systems.

Best Use Cases

High-energy variety streams

Competitive or PvP-focused games

Streams where music-driven hype moments are frequent

Channel point or event-heavy chat interactions

PRO

Tips

If flames feel too intense, reduce intensity scale or max thickness before lowering ember counts.

For music-focused streams, keep audio enabled but lower master volume so it complements game or mic audio rather than competing with it.
