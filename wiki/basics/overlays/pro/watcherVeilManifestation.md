Watcher Veil: Manifestation

A horror fog overlay that escalates into brief shadow forms, eye glimpses, and subtle edge distortion during spikes, with a controlled reveal moment at max hype. Most impactful when you want hype peaks to feel like the overlay is watching back.

Overview

Watcher Veil: Manifestation builds on the same edge-hugging fog, vignette, and breathing pulse foundation as Watcher Veil, but adds manifestations that appear within the fog. As hype rises, shadow shapes and brief eye-like glimpses become more frequent, and spikes can trigger subtle edge distortion. At max hype, a short reveal moment plays before returning to normal behavior.

Hype Behavior

Low hype behavior
Baseline fog and light vignette with minimal pulse, matching the subtle atmosphere of the Free version.

Medium hype behavior
The fog suggests presence behind it as occasional shadow ripples or implied shapes appear. The vignette deepens and the pulse becomes more noticeable.

High / spike behavior
Shadow manifestations become more frequent and more defined, eye glimpses occur more often, and spikes can trigger subtle edge distortion pulses. At max hype, a short reveal moment plays once per peak moment, then goes on cooldown.

Faction Interaction

Blended / weighted
Faction colors blend together to tint the fog subtly. Tinting is optional, but enabled by default.

Per-faction regions
Faction influence can bias where manifestations appear on the screen.

Horror-focused streams where hype peaks should feel threatening

Tier

Tips

If distortion feels distracting, reduce Distortion Intensity or disable distortion while keeping manifestations.

If you want fewer eyes, increase Shadow vs Eye or reduce Eye Glimpse Duration.

If manifestations are too frequent during busy chat, reduce Manifestation Frequency or increase Max Reveal Cooldown.
