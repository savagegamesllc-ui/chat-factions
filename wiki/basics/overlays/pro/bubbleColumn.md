# Bubble Column

A vertical stack of soft, floating bubbles that displays recent channel activity with a cute, lighthearted feel, best suited for calm streams and positive hype moments.

---

## Overview
Bubble Column presents four rounded, bubble-like panels arranged vertically. Each panel gently floats in place while faint background bubbles drift upward behind them. The panels are connected by a soft chain of smaller bubbles, giving the entire overlay a playful, airy look that feels light and friendly rather than intense.

---

## Hype Behavior
- **Low hype**  
  Panels drift slowly with minimal movement. Background bubbles rise gently, and connections remain calm and subtle.

- **Medium hype**  
  Floating motion becomes more noticeable. Bubble connections wobble slightly, panel glow increases, and shimmer effects become easier to see.

- **High / spike behavior**  
  Movement becomes more energetic while remaining soft. Additional sparkles appear, and brief heart-shaped “blups” float upward near the connecting bubbles before fading away.

---

## Faction Interaction
- **Single faction focus**  
  The leading faction controls the color tint of the panels, bubbles, and connections.
- Colors are softened into pastel tones to maintain the overlay’s light, cute aesthetic.

---

## Configuration Options
- **Anchor X**  
  Sets the horizontal position of the bubble column on screen.

- **Anchor Y**  
  Sets the vertical position of the bubble column on screen.

- **Column Height**  
  Controls how tall the full vertical stack of panels appears.

- **Panel Width**  
  Adjusts the width of each bubble panel.

- **Panel Gap**  
  Sets the spacing between panels.

- **Faction Mix**  
  Determines whether the leading faction or a weighted blend of factions controls color.

- **Intensity**  
  Scales how strongly hype affects motion and visual energy.

- **Background Bubbles**  
  Controls how many ambient bubbles float behind the panels.

- **Sparkle**  
  Adjusts the amount of small sparkle effects inside the panels and connections.

- **Enable Links**  
  Toggles the bubble-bead connections between panels.

- **Bead Size**  
  Adjusts the size of the connecting bubble beads.

- **Enable Hearts**  
  Toggles heart-shaped blups during hype spikes.

- **Heart Rate**  
  Controls how often heart blups appear during spikes.

- **Heart Size**  
  Adjusts the size of heart blups.
