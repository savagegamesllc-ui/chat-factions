EventDance

A dynamic frame that surrounds the screen with flowing motion, animated symbols, and rhythmic bursts, becoming most striking during sustained chat activity and sudden hype spikes.

Overview

EventDance presents a lively, animated frame that reacts continuously to chat energy. Flowing ribbons trace the edges of the screen, symbolic shapes occupy corners or regions, and bursts of particles appear as hype shifts, creating the feeling that the entire frame is “dancing” with the stream.

Hype Behavior

Low hype behavior
Subtle motion along the frame edges, with gentle ribbon flow and minimal particle activity. The overlay remains present but restrained.

Medium hype behavior
Ribbons move faster and appear thicker, symbolic elements begin to hop or rotate more noticeably, and particle bursts occur more frequently across different regions.

High / spike behavior
The frame becomes highly animated. Ribbons intensify, symbols gain extra motion and detail, and frequent bursts of particles erupt around the edges and corners, emphasizing major hype moments.

Faction Interaction

Blended / weighted
Colors and visual intensity reflect a weighted blend of all active factions based on their current hype levels. No single faction fully overrides the others unless explicitly configured to do so.

Configuration Options

This overlay has no user-facing configuration options.

Performance Notes

Designed to remain stable during extended hype by limiting visual density and adapting motion when activity becomes heavy. No special performance considerations were noted for this overlay.

Best Use Cases

Variety streams with frequent chat interaction

Community-driven events or celebrations

Streams that benefit from constant, rhythmic visual energy

Moments with repeated hype spikes rather than single climaxes

Tier

PRO

Tips

Works best when left on for long sessions, allowing the motion to build naturally with chat activity.

Particularly effective when multiple factions are active, as blended colors enhance the sense of movement.
