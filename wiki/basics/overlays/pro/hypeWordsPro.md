Hype Words (PRO)

Short words or phrases appear on screen with advanced variation, faction-aware coloring, and special emphasis during hype spikes.

Overview

Hype Words (PRO) expands on the base overlay with multiple phrase groups, optional faction color influence, and a brief center-screen emphasis during major hype increases.

Hype Behavior

Low hype behavior
No words appear when hype is at zero.

Medium hype behavior
Words appear regularly from enabled phrase groups, drifting and fading across the screen.

High / spike behavior
Words appear rapidly, and during sudden hype spikes a highlighted phrase briefly appears near the center before fading out.

Faction Interaction

Winner-dominant
When enabled, word coloring can reflect the leading faction’s color, with stronger influence at higher hype levels.

Configuration Options

Phrase Banks
Allows multiple groups of words to be defined and enabled or disabled independently.

Spawn Rate Scaling
Controls how quickly words appear as hype increases.

Maximum Active Words
Limits how many words may be visible at once.

Word Lifetime
Controls how long words remain visible.

Text Size Range
Defines minimum and maximum word sizes.

Faction Tint Mode
Enables or disables faction-based coloring.

Big Moment Trigger
Enables a brief, emphasized word display during hype spikes.

Performance Notes

The overlay enforces strict limits on simultaneous words and per-frame spawning to maintain stable performance during high hype.

Best Use Cases

High-energy streams

Competitive or faction-driven communities

Streams that benefit from visible hype spikes

Moments where chat surges rapidly
