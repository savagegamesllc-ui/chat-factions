Hype Words

Short words or phrases appear briefly at random positions on screen, with frequency driven entirely by chat hype.

Overview

Hype Words displays short, streamer-defined words or phrases that fade and drift across the screen. The overlay remains visually minimal and non-intrusive, adding reactive text elements only when hype is present.

Hype Behavior

Low hype behavior
No words appear when hype is at zero.

Medium hype behavior
Words begin appearing occasionally at random positions, drifting lightly before fading out.

High / spike behavior
Words appear more frequently, with multiple phrases visible at once as hype rises.

Faction Interaction

This overlay does not visually distinguish between factions.

Configuration Options

Word / Phrase Slots (up to 7)
Allows the streamer to define the words or phrases that may appear on screen.

Spawn Rate Scaling
Controls how frequently words appear as hype increases.

Maximum Active Words
Limits how many words can be visible on screen at one time.

Word Lifetime
Controls how long each word remains visible before fading out.

Text Size Range
Defines the minimum and maximum size words may appear at.

Movement Drift
Controls how words gently move while visible.

Performance Notes

The overlay limits the number of active words and removes them automatically to prevent buildup.

Best Use Cases

Tips

Leave unused word slots empty to reduce repetition.

Set base spawn to zero if you want words to appear only when hype is present.
