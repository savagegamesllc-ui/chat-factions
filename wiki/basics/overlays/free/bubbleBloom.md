Bubble Bloom

A field of soft, rising bubbles that gently fills the screen, becoming denser and more active as hype builds. It is most impactful during sustained audience engagement where hype steadily climbs.

Overview

Bubble Bloom displays translucent bubbles that rise upward across the screen with a light wobble. The overall look is calm and clean, adding motion and atmosphere without blocking the stream. Bubble color is subtly tinted toward the active faction.

Hype Behavior

Low hype behavior
Only a small number of bubbles appear, rising slowly and sparsely to keep the screen mostly clear.

Medium hype behavior
Bubble spawn rate increases and motion becomes more noticeable, creating a fuller and more lively field of bubbles.

High / spike behavior
Bubbles appear frequently and in greater numbers, with faster upward movement, creating a dense and energetic visual effect.

Faction Interaction

Winner-dominant
Bubbles tint toward the leading faction’s color when one faction is clearly ahead.

Blended / weighted
When enabled, bubble color blends based on faction meter weights.

No per-faction regions or isolated faction zones are used.

Configuration Options

Faction Mix
Choose whether bubble color follows the leading faction or a weighted blend of all factions.

Intensity
Controls the overall strength of the effect, influencing how strongly hype affects bubble activity.

FPS Cap
Limits the maximum frame rate used by the overlay.

Render Scale
Adjusts internal rendering resolution to balance visual quality and performance.

Max Bubbles
Sets the maximum number of bubbles allowed on screen at once.

Base Spawn Rate
Defines how many bubbles appear when hype is very low.

Spawn Boost
Controls how much additional bubble spawning occurs as hype increases.

Minimum Size
Sets the smallest possible bubble size.

Maximum Size
Sets the largest possible bubble size.

Tint Strength
Determines how strongly faction color influences the bubble appearance.

Edge Fade
Fades bubbles near the edges of the screen to reduce visual clutter.

Hype Scale
Controls how quickly hype translates into visible intensity.

Max Total Clamp
Limits how much total hype can influence the overlay.

Hype Smoothing
Adjusts how quickly visual changes respond to hype updates.

Performance Notes

Higher bubble counts and larger render scales increase GPU usage. Lowering render scale or maximum bubbles can improve performance on lower-end systems.
