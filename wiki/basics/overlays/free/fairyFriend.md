# Fairy Friend

A small fairy companion floats freely around the screen, occasionally speaking short phrases while glowing brighter as hype builds.

---

## Overview
Fairy Friend is a roaming companion overlay inspired by a helpful but easily excitable guide. The fairy drifts organically around the screen, never anchoring to a fixed UI element, giving the impression of an aware, living presence rather than a static decoration. At random moments, a small speech bubble appears near the fairy with brief phrases before fading away.

---

## Hype Behavior
- **Low hype behavior**  
  The fairy remains small and softly lit, drifting calmly with occasional idle speech bubbles.

- **Medium hype behavior**  
  The fairy gradually grows in size and glow intensity, moving with slightly more energy as hype increases.

- **High / spike behavior**  
  The fairy reaches its largest size and brightest glow, clearly reflecting heightened excitement without overwhelming the screen.

---

## Faction Interaction
- **Winner-dominant**  
  The fairy’s glow color always reflects the current hype-leading faction.

---

## Configuration Options
- **Sub Phrase**  
  A custom phrase that appears only when a subscription event occurs.

- **Bits Phrase**  
  A custom phrase that appears only when bits are sent.

Speech bubbles are protected by cooldowns and will never overlap or override an active message.

---

## Performance Notes
No special performance considerations were noted for this overlay.

---

## Best Use Cases
- Variety streams
- Chill or cozy streams
- Chat-focused streams
- Streams that benefit from light personality and humor without visual clutter

**FREE**

## Tips
- Keep custom sub and bits phrases short for best readability.
- This overlay works especially well when paired with more subtle edge or border-based visuals.
