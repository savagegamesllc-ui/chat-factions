# 70s Game Show

A bold, animated border that surrounds the stream with colorful motion and energetic glow, evoking classic television game shows with a modern digital twist.

---

## Overview

70s Game Show frames the screen with layered, colorful outlines that continuously animate around the edges of the stream. The effect is vibrant, playful, and attention-grabbing, designed to feel lively even when hype is low.

The border leans into retro spectacle rather than subtlety, making it ideal for streams that want a fun, high-energy visual identity.

---

## Hype Behavior

Hype influences brightness, speed, and overall intensity of the border.

At low hype:
- Motion is steady and decorative.
- Colors cycle smoothly without overpowering the scene.

As hype increases:
- Animation speed increases.
- Glow intensity ramps up.
- Pulsing becomes more noticeable, giving the frame a celebratory feel.

Hype changes scale smoothly, avoiding hard jumps or sudden visual shocks.

---

## Event Reactions

- **Bits**
  - Trigger a temporary boost in brightness and animation speed.
  - The border feels energized but remains playful and readable.

- **Subscriptions**
  - Cause a strong visual surge across the entire frame.
  - All layers brighten simultaneously before settling back into normal motion.

These reactions are short-lived and designed to feel festive rather than dramatic.

---

## Visual Elements

- **Animated Border Layers**
  - Multiple outlines create depth and motion.
  - Colors cycle continuously around the frame.

- **Glow Effects**
  - Soft bloom around the border enhances visibility.
  - Glow strength increases with hype and events.

- **Retro Motion Style**
  - Movement favors smooth loops and repetition.
  - Overall aesthetic feels classic and upbeat rather than futuristic.

---

## Placement & Use

70s Game Show is designed to frame the entire stream canvas and works best on layouts where a bold border enhances the overall presentation.

It is especially well-suited for:
- Variety streams
- Talk shows
- Community-focused or casual streams
- Retro-themed content
