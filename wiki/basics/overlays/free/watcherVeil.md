Watcher Veil

A subtle horror atmosphere of edge-hugging fog and a deepening vignette that becomes more noticeable as hype rises, without overtaking the center of the screen. Most impactful when you want a persistent eerie mood that still keeps gameplay readable.

Overview

Watcher Veil is an edge-weighted fog/smoke overlay with a soft vignette and gentle “breathing” pulses. The fog drifts organically along the borders and can carry a subtle blended faction tint, while the center is kept clearer for readability.

Hype Behavior

Low hype behavior
Fog is barely visible with slow drift and a light vignette. The breathing pulse is minimal and soft.

Medium hype behavior
Fog becomes clearly present along the edges, drift speed increases slightly, and the vignette deepens. The breathing pulse becomes more noticeable while staying smooth.

High / spike behavior
Fog reaches its thickest edge presence and the vignette is strongest. Breathing pulses feel heavier and occur a bit more often, while the center remains relatively clear.

Faction Interaction

Blended / weighted
Faction colors blend together to tint the fog subtly. Tinting is optional, but enabled by default.

Vignette Strength

Vignette Softness

Pulse Strength

Faction Tinting

Tint Strength

Center Clear Bias

Tips

If the fog feels too intrusive, reduce Fog Inset or increase Center Clear Bias.

If faction tint stands out too much for the horror mood, lower Tint Strength while leaving tinting enabled.
