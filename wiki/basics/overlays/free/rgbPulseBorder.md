# RGB Pulse Border

A full-screen RGB border that reacts to hype with increasingly energetic blinking and pulsing, anchored by a solid inner LED line showing the current hype leader.

---

## Overview

RGB Pulse Border wraps the entire stream view in a clean, modern frame designed to feel like programmable LED hardware. The outer border displays a full RGB spectrum that blinks in a random, organic pattern, while an inner border provides a steady visual anchor tied directly to the leading faction.

At low hype levels the effect is subtle and ambient. As hype grows, the border becomes more alive, signaling rising energy without obscuring gameplay or camera space.

---

## Hype Behavior

Hype directly influences both motion and intensity.

At low hype:
- The outer RGB border blinks slowly and irregularly.
- Pulsing is minimal and soft.
- The inner LED border remains stable and readable.

At higher hype:
- Outer border blinking becomes faster and more frequent.
- A rhythmic pulse emerges, expanding and contracting brightness.
- Glow and intensity increase smoothly rather than jumping between states.
- The inner LED border brightens and gains presence while remaining clean.

All transitions scale smoothly with hype rather than using fixed tiers.

---

## Visual Elements

- **Outer RGB Border**
  - Displays a continuous RGB color cycle.
  - Individual segments blink independently to avoid uniform flashing.
  - Motion feels randomized but controlled, similar to addressable LED strips.

- **Inner LED Border**
  - Always matches the current hype leader’s color.
  - Thinner and more focused than the outer border.
  - Enhanced glow at higher hype levels to reinforce leadership.

- **Glow & Pulse**
  - Glow intensity increases with hype.
  - Pulsing is layered on top of blinking, creating depth rather than noise.

---

## Placement & Use

RGB Pulse Border is designed to frame the entire stream canvas.

It works especially well for:
- Competitive team-based games
- Events with frequent hype swings
- Streams where chat engagement is a primary focus

The border scales cleanly across resolutions and aspect ratios, making it suitable for both fullscreen gameplay and camera-heavy layouts.
