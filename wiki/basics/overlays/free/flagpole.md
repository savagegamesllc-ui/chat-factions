# Flagpole

A retro-inspired flagpole where the flag slowly rises as hype builds, evoking the end-of-level moment from classic platformers.

---

## Overview

Flagpole displays a vertical flagpole with a fabric flag attached near the top. The flag gently waves at all times, creating a sense of motion even at low activity. As total hype increases across all factions, the flag is raised higher up the pole, providing a clear visual indicator of overall engagement.

The flag’s color always reflects the current hype leader, allowing viewers to instantly see which faction is in control.

---

## Hype Behavior

Total hype across all active factions determines how high the flag is raised.

Low hype keeps the flag near the bottom of the pole. As hype grows, the flag steadily climbs upward, approaching the top at high hype levels. Changes in hype are smoothed so the movement feels natural rather than abrupt.

The leading faction controls the flag’s color at all times.

---

## Visual Behavior

The flag continuously waves as if caught in a light breeze, regardless of hype level. This motion is subtle and constant, designed to feel calm rather than chaotic.

The pole remains static while the flag’s vertical position and color update in response to hype changes.

---

## Customization

Streamers can adjust the flagpole’s placement on the screen, the size of the flag, the speed and strength of the wind effect, and how quickly the flag responds to hype changes.

These settings allow the overlay to be subtle and decorative or more prominent, depending on the stream layout.

---

## Availability

Flagpole is available as a Free overlay.

This overlay can be upgraded to a Pro version with enhanced visual effects while retaining the same core behavior.
