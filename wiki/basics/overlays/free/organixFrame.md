# Organix Frame

An organic, living-tech label frame that sits cleanly on the screen to display streamer stats while pulsing brighter with rising hype.

---

## Overview

Organix Frame is a compact animated label bar designed to frame important on-stream information such as the latest follower or subscriber. Its shape and motion resemble grown technology rather than mechanical UI, with soft organic contours and subtle internal motion that make it feel alive even at low hype.

The frame remains readable at all times, with all motion and color activity contained within the surrounding structure rather than the text itself.

---

## Hype Behavior

As hype increases, Organix Frame responds by gradually brightening and pulsing in intensity. The frame’s glow expands and contracts in a smooth rhythm that becomes more energetic at higher hype levels.

The frame color shifts toward the leading faction’s color, flowing through the organic channels of the frame rather than snapping instantly. When faction meters are close, blended colors may remain visible, reflecting ongoing competition.

At higher hype moments, the pulsing becomes more pronounced while staying controlled and non-intrusive.

---

## Best Use Cases

Organix Frame works especially well for:

- Latest Follower
- Latest Subscriber
- Top Gifter
- Custom informational labels

Its compact size and stable text presentation make it ideal for persistent on-screen placement such as lower-thirds or corner displays without interfering with gameplay or camera feeds.

---

## Notes

Organix Frame is a Free tier overlay focused on clarity, usability, and subtle visual impact. All animation and color changes are confined to the frame itself, ensuring that text remains easy to read during both calm and high-energy moments.
